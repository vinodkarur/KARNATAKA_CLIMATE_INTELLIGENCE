IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
1. These results describe the supplied Karnataka dataset.
2. Correlation does not establish causation.
3. Monthly anomalies remove average calendar-month seasonality, but do not remove all weather or climate influences.
4. Kruskal-Wallis and ordinary correlation p-values do not account for serial autocorrelation.
5. Annual linear trend p-values may also be affected by temporal autocorrelation.
6. ENSO classifications are based on the historical ONI five-overlapping-season threshold rule and do not independently verify atmospheric conditions.
7. Centered three-month ONI values are for retrospective analysis and must not be used as future-known forecast inputs.
